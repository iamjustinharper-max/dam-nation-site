Dam Clan Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Dam Clan ("the game") is published by Prism Bear Group Ltd ("we"). This policy explains what information the game handles and why.

What the game stores on your device

Your game progress (the colony, its river, research and settings) is saved on your device only. It is never uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

The Android version shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and information about the device and how you interact with ads. In the UK and EEA you will be asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown; you can choose non-personalised ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchase

The one-time "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to verify purchases and restore them on your other devices. RevenueCat receives a random app-user ID, purchase receipts and device platform information; it does not receive your name or email. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we do not do

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. We do not sell personal information. The game has no accounts, chat or user-generated content.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can withdraw ad consent in the game's settings (the ? button) or by resetting your advertising ID. You can stop all data handling by uninstalling the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: justin@prismbeargroup.com

Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and note the date above.
